Projects

Gmail Attachment Automation

Click to enlarge

Problem: Manually monitoring emails across multiple Gmail labels, recording sender information, and organizing attachments in Google Drive is repetitive and time-consuming. Repeated executions can also create duplicate folders.

Solution: Built a self-hosted n8n workflow using Docker that monitors Gmail on a scheduled interval, extracts sender information into Google Sheets, and automatically downloads email attachments into a structured Google Drive hierarchy organized by label, date, and sender. Implemented conditional find-or-create logic to prevent duplicate folders across repeated runs.

Tech Stack: n8n, Docker, Gmail API, Google Sheets API, Google Drive API

Lead Generation Automation

Click to enlarge

Problem: Finding business leads and following up with each one by hand is slow, and it's easy to lose track of who has been contacted.

Solution: An n8n workflow pulls leads from a website into Google Sheets, enriches each one through an API request, and uses an If check to decide who gets a personalized Gmail message. The sheet row is updated after every lead, so the sheet always shows where each contact stands.

Tech stack: n8n, HTTP/API requests, JavaScript, Gmail, Google Sheets

AI Reel Video Automation

Click to enlarge

Problem: Turning a raw video into a captioned short-form reel means editing every clip by hand, which takes a lot of time.

Solution: An n8n workflow picks the next video from a Google Sheet, downloads it from Google Drive, and has Gemini analyze it. It trims, mutes and blurs as needed, adds an AI voiceover with Google Cloud text-to-speech, generates captions as a subtitle file, assembles the final reel, and uploads it back to Drive.

Tech stack: n8n, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Gemini AI, Google Cloud TTS

Inventory Ledger System

Click to enlarge

Problem: Business partners funding and selling inventory had to work out cash on hand, unsold stock and each person's profit share by hand.

Solution: A desktop app with a dashboard that tracks capital contributions and withdrawals, unsold stock cost and cash on hand. It splits profits using each ledger's percentages (like 60/40), keeps a payout history, and exports to Excel.

Tech stack: React, Electron, Strapi

POS & Inventory System (Capstone)

Click to enlarge
